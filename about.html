Rooted in Jalgaon: Our Story

Siddhi Organics was born out of a deep-rooted passion for sustainable farming and an unwavering commitment to healing the soil from the ground up. Operating directly from our farm facility in Wavadade, Jalgaon, we witnessed firsthand how chemical-heavy agriculture was rapidly depleting organic carbon and draining local soil vitality. We knew that true, long-lasting crop health could only be restored by turning back to nature.

Driven by technical agricultural expertise—led by our Managing Director Sahebrao Laxman Phuse (Bari) and Founder & Agricultural Technologist Mayur Sahebrao Phuse (Bari) (B.Sc. Agriculture)—we turned to nature's original recyclers: earthworms. Today, Siddhi Organics stands as an independent, producer-direct enterprise overseeing every step of bio-conversion to bring 100% pure earth solutions to cultivators across Maharashtra.
